⚡ Multi-Model Compute Topology & Throughput (TPS)

Comparing token generation speed across 8B dense, Gemma 4, GLM-5.2 (744B MoE), and DeepSeek-R1 (671B MoE) across CPU disk-streaming, RAM-resident, single/dual L4 GPUs, and 8x H100 multi-GPU clusters.

Multi-Model Architectural Insights

  • DeepSeek-R1 (671B MoE): Requires 37B active parameters per forward pass. On 8x H100 80GB nodes (FP8/INT4 quantization), it achieves 350–480 TPS under dynamic batching, unlocking real-time reasoning.
  • GLM-5.2 (744B MoE): Utilizes 40B active parameters across 256 routed experts. Requires high-capacity 8x GPU nodes to maintain 400+ TPS under dynamic enterprise batching.
  • Gemma 4 (27B / 9B): Google's Gemma 4 family operates efficiently on 1–2x L4 GPUs (`g2-standard-12`) or single A100s, delivering 90 TPS single-stream and 600+ TPS under heavy batching.
  • 8B Dense Models: Provide single-digit millisecond latency (75–500 TPS) on basic single L4 GPUs ($0.70/hr), ideal for high-concurrency microservices.
